Define the route by required waypoints, headings, stops and obstacles. Treat the drawn curve as a starting guess, and let Optimize choose the shape between requirements.

Accepted owner decision — 17 October

The owner explicitly chose Choose the route. The requested improvement concerns arbitrary path-deviation tuning, not removal of robot physics. Users should not need to choose a distance from their sketch to get a useful route. Hiding the input, renaming the same radius Automatic or silently increasing it does not meet this contract.

Starting point

The committed predecessor varies existing Bézier handle lengths, preserving their directions, inside an authored corridor. It validates the drawn curve before search and requires its portal sequence. The UI exposes the corridor as Path freedom and draws a band. Thus a bad sketch can block a task that is actually feasible.

Accepted version1 identity, project validation and export validation also require the old corridor. A free-route search cannot be introduced solely by removing the control or one worker check. The planner, candidate ownership, comparison, artifact identity and independent acceptance must agree on the chosen task.

Required behavior

  1. Preserve required ordered positions, authored heading requirements, stops, waits and event semantics. Shape and handles supply a seed rather than a movement envelope.
  2. Find a validated route when the seed is poor or collides but the required task remains feasible. An impossible required pose produces an explicit failure, not a fake no-gain success.
  3. Retain physical/dense validation, actual field and footprint clearance, height and portal rules. Seed deviation is not a substitute for those checks, and removing deviation cannot weaken them.
  4. Preview the candidate and apply only explicitly. Preserve exact applied samples/timing through playback, routines, source save/reload and BDX export. Cancellation, deadlines and stale documents never apply a result.
  5. Do not silently reinterpret existing paths or discard corridor values and version1 artifacts. Existing applied paths continue under their original validation until explicit reoptimization and Apply under the new model.

Implemented working-tree direction

A separate bounded route search uses required waypoints, full-vector handle candidates, obstacle/portal visibility guidance and internal solver knots. Required point order and heading/action semantics remain attached to their original points. Intent identity is separate from full seed/job identity. New accepted version2 artifacts retain exact geometry and samples; project1.2 and standalone path2.1 prevent older readers silently reinterpreting them. Legacy version1 validation and an explicit archive remain intact.

The panel removes deviation/corridor authoring and distinguishes valid, invalid and unknown seed qualification. An unqualified seed does not produce a percentage gain claim. Apply uses lightweight structural staging; workers and export perform independent numerical acceptance. Clearance certifies the oriented footprint along exported pose interpolation with finite work and explicit unknown outcomes. Authored limits remain; the review rejected an invented automatic BUMP speed policy and it has been removed.

The current finite search supports Bézier paths with2–41required points and no jiggle. Unsupported kinds get a concrete reason. It is not a global-optimum or Choreo-equivalence claim.

Verification target

Use cases must include a poor seed needing more movement than the old corridor cap, obstacle detours from an invalid seed, impossible required poses, required waypoint/heading/stop order, mixed event and zone anchors, existing applied legacy files, new Apply/save/reload/export equality, cancellation/deadline ownership and actual rendered optimizer states. Numerical correctness and usable output are required; no completion claim from a removed slider. All current benchmark and hardware limits remain explicit.
