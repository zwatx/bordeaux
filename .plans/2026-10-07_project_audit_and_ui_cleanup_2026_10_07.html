Remaining risks and follow-ups

  • The canonical index adds O(samples) allocation. Dense coincident queries and distant fallback queries can retain O(waypoints × samples) work. The benchmark repair does not establish globally linear planning, peak memory or a before/after end-to-end speedup.
  • Native Windows filesystem/installer behavior, NI automation, physical robot transport/runtime, release signatures, and hardware timing are not verified here. Mocked Windows UI fixtures do not establish native platform support.
  • Follow-up #208 tracks eight moderate affected entries in the development packaging chain for GHSA-hp3w-g68c-fv3c. No runtime exploit path was established. The suggested automated fix downgrades electron-builder across versions; do not perform that unrelated downgrade without compatibility work.
  • The raw IPC save-error prefix, wrapped stat-tile units, minor source-only visual hypotheses, legacy zero-based waypoint-zone label, large Electron orchestrator and unused/general deployment infrastructure remain future cleanup work. Keyboard-only scrolling of the extreme title and full screen-reader coverage were not verified. This audit does not justify a sweeping rewrite of stable boundaries.
  • Several older UI tracker findings appear fixed or overlap current work. They are reconciled in audit notes; no issue is closed against an uncommitted patch.
